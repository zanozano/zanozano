## 🚀 About Me

Hello! I'm **Cristobal Manzano (@zanozano)**. I am a **Product Engineer** with over 10 years of experience blurring the lines between user experience and full-stack development. I don't separate design from code. I believe great digital products require an unbroken thread from the first user interview to the final database query.

I specialize in building scalable web applications with intuitive interfaces, robust architectures, and design systems that actually work in production.

*   🏙️ **Base of operations:** Hamamatsu, Japan.
*   🧠 **Current focus:** Accelerating **Design System** implementation and scalability through AI automation and emerging technologies.
*   🤝 **Looking for:** Product-driven teams building innovative, high-impact digital experiences.
*   💬 **Let's talk about:** Component-driven development, user psychology, and end-to-end product ownership.

## 💡 Product Philosophy

*   🔄 **Holistic Vision:** I understand product development from end to end. My experience in frontend and user experience allows me to make sounder decisions and seamlessly connect business needs with technical implementation.
*   ⚡ **Reality-Driven Design:** I use Figma with a developer mindset. I design solutions that are fully aligned with technical capabilities and limitations from day one, eliminating rework and ensuring a seamless handoff to development.
*   👥 **Collaborative Approach:** I thrive on collaborating with cross-functional teams and transforming complex requirements into clear, scalable, and executable solutions.
